HEALTHCARE · CASE STUDYIn production since 2019

An electronic health records platform for a US residential care provider

A US provider of residential, group-home care needed one system for client records, nursing assessments, care plans and daily shift logs, with strict access control and a full audit trail. I have led its development since 2019, from the first commit to today.

The problem

Residential care providers look after people around the clock across several homes. Nurses run regular assessments, write care plans that staff must follow and sign, and every shift has to be recorded. When that lives in paper binders and spreadsheets, assessments go overdue, care plans drift out of date, and it’s hard to prove to auditors who did what and when.

The provider needed a single, secure system that care staff, nurses and coordinators could all use, which would also flag what was due before it became overdue.

What made it hard

  • Sensitive health data. Every record had to be access-controlled by role, changes tracked, and deletions recoverable.
  • Clinical detail. A nursing assessment alone has around twenty sections, from vitals and lab work to system reviews and staff supervision.
  • Care plans change. Plans are revised often, and staff need to see exactly what changed and acknowledge the new version.
  • A system that keeps growing. New forms and rules arrive every year, so changes had to ship without disrupting daily care.

What I built

  • Client records. Demographics, contacts, insurance, providers, ICD diagnoses, medications, allergies, diet, immunisations, tests and program enrolment in one profile, printable as a PDF.
  • Nursing assessments and care plans. Multi-section assessments with a dashboard, and care plans covering risk areas, trained staff and progress notes, with versioning and a side-by-side history of what changed.
  • Record of Service. Per-shift logs of food intake, health observations, seizures and nurse notifications, with QA reviewer sign-off.
  • Automated reminders. Daily background jobs find each client’s latest assessment, skip discharged clients, and email staff when a review window opens or an assessment is overdue, using thresholds the provider configures. Each send is recorded, and operations get a summary after every run.
  • Access and audit. A permission tree of 392 permissions, full audit logging, change history on clinical records, soft delete, and sign-in only from approved workstations.
  • Performance. Debounced searches and removed duplicate requests on heavy list screens, plus Redis caching.

How it fits together

  1. Referral and intakeReferrals, contacts, needs and documents for a new client
  2. Client recordDiagnoses, medications, allergies, insurance, providers and programs
  3. NursingAssessments, care plans with version history, notes and incident reports
  4. Daily careRecord of Service per shift, with QA reviewer sign-off
  5. OversightDue-date reminders, audit logs, reports and approved workstations

The result

  • Client records, nursing work and daily shift logs live in one system, instead of binders and spreadsheets.
  • Overdue assessments are flagged automatically, so nurses act before deadlines rather than after audits.
  • Every change to a clinical record is traceable to a person and a time, which supports compliance reviews.
  • The platform has stayed in production for more than six years while growing through 270+ database migrations.
