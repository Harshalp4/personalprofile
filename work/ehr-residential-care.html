One EHR for US care providers, from daily care to lab results

US residential and behavioral care providers were running client care on paper binders, spreadsheets, faxes and phone calls. I lead the engineering of the EHR platform that replaced that, now used by several providers, with LabCorp-certified lab integration and electronic pharmacy refills.

The problem

Residential and behavioral care providers look after people around the clock across many homes. Before the platform, their work lived in paper binders and spreadsheets. Medication records were hard to check, assessments slipped past their due dates, and care plans were out of date by the time staff read them.

Labs and pharmacies made it worse. Lab requisitions were printed and faxed, results arrived as paper or PDFs someone had to file by hand, and refills were confirmed over the phone. Proving to auditors who did what, and when, took days.

What we delivered

  • One record per person. Health history, medications, allergies, contacts, insurance and programs in a single profile that every authorised staff member can see.
  • Medications and daily care. Electronic medication administration, shift logs and incident reports, recorded as care happens.
  • Nursing and behavioral care. Assessments, care plans with a clear history of every change, and behavioral plans, all in one place.
  • Lab orders and results with LabCorp. Staff order lab tests from the EHR, and results come back into the person’s record automatically. The integration completed LabCorp’s certification before going live.
  • Electronic pharmacy refills. Refill requests arrive in the system and wait for a nurse’s approval, instead of phone calls and manual entry.
  • Reminders and audit trail. Automatic alerts before assessments become overdue, role-based access, and a record of every change.
  • Built to grow. One shared platform serves several providers, each with the forms and rules they need.

How it fits together

  1. Client recordOne profile per person: health history, medications, contacts and programs
  2. Daily careMedication administration, shift logs and incident reports
  3. Clinical careNursing assessments, care plans and behavioral plans
  4. Labs and pharmacyLab orders and results with LabCorp; pharmacy refills approved by nurses
  5. OversightReminders, reports and a full audit trail

How it helped

  • Paperwork replaced. Care, medications and clinical notes are recorded in one system instead of binders and spreadsheets.
  • Faster lab results. No faxed requisitions or hand-filed reports: results reach the right person’s record automatically.
  • Fewer missed deadlines. Staff are reminded before assessments fall overdue, not after an audit finds them.
  • Audit-ready. Every change is traceable to a person and a time, which makes compliance reviews quicker.
  • Trusted long term. In daily use for more than six years, with new providers added over time.
